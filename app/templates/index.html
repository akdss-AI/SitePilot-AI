What it does

Materials & stock alerts

Know what is on site, what arrived, and what is about to run out — with low-stock alerts computed from real quantities.

Tasks & overdue follow-up

Deadlines, tasks due today, and what is coming up in the next 7 days — overdue items surface until they are done.

Site issues

Issues by category and priority, critical and high first, so the urgent ones never hide at the bottom of a list.

LLM site updates

Describe the day in ordinary words. SitePilot proposes structured changes, validates them, and only applies what is permitted — never raw SQL, never an unvalidated write.

Daily summary

One view per project, generated fresh each morning from live data: delays, shortages, open issues, upcoming tasks, pending deliveries.
